## Brand & Style

The design system is crafted for high-velocity social commerce sellers, live streamers, and affiliate marketers operating across platforms like TikTok Shop, Shopee, and LINE Shopping. It balances the ultra-refined, structured engineering aesthetic of developer-first platforms with the dynamic energy, urgency, and monetization clarity required in modern e-commerce.

The visual style blends **Modern Hyper-Clean SaaS** with **Tactile Micro-Surfacing**:
- **Pure Canvas Precision:** Deep contrast anchored on pure white containers with cool-slate borders and pale neutral canvas backings.
- **Action-Oriented Energy:** High-intent utility driven by electric cobalt primary controls, balanced with vibrant commerce amber for checkout and affiliate triggers.
- **Bilingual Typographic Harmony:** Built for seamless visual weight matching between English and Thai scripts, preventing vertical clipping of tone marks and diacritics.
- **High Information Density with Generous Air:** Crisp borders and compact data tables sit within relaxed structural viewports, providing clarity under complex inventory, analytics, and automation workflows.

## Colors

The palette operates on a strict functional hierarchy built for daylight readability, scanability, and financial confidence:

- **Canvas & Surface Architecture:**
  - `canvas-default`: `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50) establishes the global viewport backdrop.
  - `surface-card`: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White) houses all focal content, tables, and component containers.
  - `surface-subtle`: `#F1F5F9` (Slate 100) provides recessed framing for search bars, disabled inputs, and pill backings.
  - `border-subtle`: `#E2E8F0` (Slate 200) serves as the universal perimeter for components and cards.
  - `border-strong`: `#CBD5E1` (Slate 300) applied on interactive hover boundaries and active tabs.

- **Brand & Action Tiers:**
  - `primary` (`#2563EB`): Electric Cobalt for core SaaS navigation, system triggers, automated sync actions, and focal CTAs. Hover state deepens to `#1D4ED8`.
  - `secondary` (`#F97316`): Commerce Amber-Orange, dedicated to flash deals, TikTok/Shopee GMV callouts, live stream notifications, and cart checkout conversions.
  - `tertiary` (`#0077C8`): PromptPay Cyan-Blue, reserved for settlement dashboards, payout confirmations, bank transfers, and financial ledger status.
  - `tertiary-dark` (`#003D6B`): National e-payment anchoring shade for bank reconciliation badges and institutional data.

- **Content & Typography:**
  - `text-primary`: `#0F172A` (Slate 900) for headers, values, and high-priority labels.
  - `text-secondary`: `#475569` (Slate 600) for body narrative and metadata.
  - `text-muted`: `#94A3B8` (Slate 400) for captions, placeholders, and inactive icons.

## Typography

The type scale strictly accounts for bilingual English/Thai typography. When rendering Thai copy, fall back systematically to **Prompt** or **Kanit** with native rendering behaviors:

- **Thai Script Rendering Rules:**
  - Standard English line-height multiples (1.2–1.3x) lead to severe vertical truncation of upper tone marks (ไม้เอก, ไม้โท) and lower vowels (สระอุ, สระอู). The typography tokens enforce a minimum line-height of 1.6x to 1.75x across all body and display sizes to ensure flawless vertical clearance.
  - Set `text-rendering: optimizeLegibility` and disable negative letter-spacing when Thai unicode ranges (`U+0E00` to `U+0E7F`) are active.
  - Heading levels pair `Inter` (weight 600/700) with `Prompt` (weight 600) for balanced character x-heights.
- **Tabular Numerics:** All currency figures, GMV metrics, and affiliate commission tallies must use `font-feature-settings: "tnum" on, "cv05" on` to align monetary data in tables.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column fluid grid on desktop transitioning to a 4-column system on mobile viewports.

- **Breakpoints:**
  - **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12 columns, max-width `1440px`, centered with `margin: 2rem` and `gutter: 1.5rem`.
  - **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8 columns, fluid edges with `margin: 1.5rem` and `gutter: 1rem`.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `margin-mobile: 1rem` and `gutter-mobile: 1rem`. Navigation collapses into a sticky bottom application dock.
- **Rhythm & Alignments:**
  - Dense dashboards leverage a 4px/8px micro-grid. Component padding scales predictably: inputs and chips use `space-sm` vertically and `space-md` horizontally.
  - Metric boards and video rendering queues utilize standard multi-row cards separated by `space-lg`.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy drop shadows, instead using crisp physical isolation via **subtle perimeter lines** and **diffused ambient micro-shadows**:

- **Level 0 (Flat/Base):** `background: #F8FAFC`, no shadow, zero elevation. Used for root canvas and sub-navigation tracks.
- **Level 1 (Card / Container):** `background: #FFFFFF`, border: `1px solid #E2E8F0`, shadow: `0 1px 2px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 3px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`. Used for analytics charts, table shells, and video queue items.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating / Dropdowns):** `background: #FFFFFF`, border: `1px solid #CBD5E1`, shadow: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`. Used for filter flyouts, popovers, and hovered cards.
- **Level 3 (Modals / Overlays):** `background: #FFFFFF`, border: `1px solid #E2E8F0`, shadow: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`. Accompanied by a backdrop blur scrim: `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.3)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`.
- **Level 4 (Sticky Dynamic Bar):** Subtle glass surface: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and a bottom border of `1px solid #E2E8F0`.

## Shapes

The geometric identity relies on soft, modern radii (`rounded-2xl` for major structural cards) offset by slightly tighter radii on actionable sub-elements:

- **Cards & Data Panels:** `1rem` (16px) for standard modules; `1.5rem` (24px) for hero containers, metric overview cards, and media playback docks.
- **Interactive Controls (Buttons, Text Inputs, Selects):** `0.5rem` (8px) ensures structural balance without excessive pill curving, keeping the tool professional and precise.
- **Badges, Tags & Status Pills:** Full pill (`9999px`) for channel tags (TikTok Shop, Shopee, PromptPay) and live indicator chips.
